# Heritage imagery

The collage in `src/content/landing-sections/heritage.md` uses representative Unsplash photos of cacao cultivation, harvest, drying, and chocolate. They are stock images, not photographs of Origen Tolima's own growers or its farm in Tolima.

Each source was checked for its cacao subject and free Unsplash License, and every selected image was visually inspected on 2026-09-17.
