## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: full-bleed photographic background for the hero of the premium cacao brand Origen Tolima. This image is only the photograph, not a website mockup.
Create a beautiful cinematic editorial still life of cacao: one large richly ridged reddish-brown ripe cacao pod resting diagonally on a dark weathered cacao-brown surface, a second pod cut open with believable creamy ivory cacao seeds, and a few dried cacao beans nearby. Entire composition feels grounded, tactile and intimate, like a premium food magazine photograph shot with a real camera.
Composition: wide landscape 16:9. Subjects occupy the middle band of the frame, with the large whole pod predominantly center-right. Quiet very dark negative space in the upper fifth for navigation and lower third for large cream website headline overlay. Keep important subjects within the central 60 percent so the composition can crop gracefully on a phone. The pods are the visual focus, no clutter or extra food.
Lighting: warm late-afternoon light rakes in from upper left, describing the sculpted ridges and natural rough skin, deep soft shadows, faint highlights on the beans, restrained contrast, subtle analog film grain. Rich cocoa brown, burnt umber, warm ochre and ivory, no bright green.
Real materials, botanical realism, sophisticated art direction, no plastic CGI shine, no excessive steam or haze. No people, hands, packaging, labels, typography, lettering, borders, logos or watermarks.
